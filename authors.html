Call for Papers

IEEE AFRICON 2027 invites submissions of original, unpublished research papers from researchers, engineers, and practitioners from universities, industry, and research institutions worldwide. The conference theme is "Harnessing Emerging Technologies for Africa's sustainable development".

Conference Tracks

Submissions should be directed to one of the eight thematic tracks below. Each track covers a focused area of engineering and technology with relevance to Africa's development agenda. Authors should select the track that best aligns with the primary contribution of their paper.

Paper Submission

All papers must be submitted electronically through the Microsoft CMT paper management system. Submitted papers must be original work not previously published or currently under review for any other conference or journal.

Papers should be written in English and must comply with the IEEE two-column format. The maximum paper length is 8 pages (including references). Authors may purchase up to 2 additional pages at an extra charge.

Author Guidelines

Paper Format

  • Papers must be formatted using the IEEE two-column conference template.
  • Use the official IEEE conference template selector for Microsoft Word or LaTeX.
  • Maximum length: 8 pages including all figures, tables, and references.
  • Up to 2 additional pages may be purchased during registration.
  • All submissions must be in PDF format.

Review Process

All papers will undergo double-anonymous peer review by a minimum of two independent reviewers. Authors must remove names, affiliations, acknowledgements and other identifying information from the review manuscript. Reviewers and committee members must declare conflicts of interest; conflicted reviewers will not be assigned to the paper. Submissions will also be screened for similarity and publication ethics concerns before a final decision is made.

Accepted authors must submit the final paper through the announced camera-ready workflow, complete the IEEE electronic Copyright Form (eCF), and provide an IEEE Xplore-compliant PDF. The conference's PDF eXpress or PDF Checker instructions and conference ID will be issued with acceptance notifications. See IEEE Xplore PDF requirements.

Publication

Papers accepted and presented in accordance with the conference presentation policy will be submitted to IEEE for consideration for inclusion in IEEE Xplore. Acceptance does not guarantee publication or indexing. At least one author of each accepted paper must register and present the work. The conference is currently planned as an in-person event; any virtual participation option will be announced only after approval.
