Current Status

No IEEE AFRICON 2027 travel-support programme is currently open and no funding is guaranteed. The organizing committee is investigating possible programmes, but eligibility, application dates, award values and available budgets depend on the applicable 2027 rules and formal approvals. Do not make travel commitments on the assumption that an award will be available.

IEEE Region 8 Voluntary Contribution Fund

IEEE Region 8's Voluntary Contribution Fund has historically supported eligible low-income IEEE members with travel and subsistence for accepted contributions at qualifying Region 8 events. AFRICON 2027 participation, eligibility and application instructions remain subject to the 2027 VCF rules. Review the official IEEE Region 8 VCF page for authoritative information. This page is not an offer of funding.

Other Programmes Under Investigation

  • IEEE Women in Engineering travel grants
  • IEEE Region 8 Professional and Educational Activities speaker support
  • IEEE Region 8 Standards-related programme support
  • IEEE Society, Council and Chapter support

These programmes are listed only as areas being investigated. They are not AFRICON 2027 funding opportunities unless a later official call appears on this website with confirmed rules and contacts.

Future Application Process

If support is confirmed, this page will publish the funding source, eligibility criteria, required documents, selection method, conflicts process, deadlines, notification date, payment or reimbursement method, and a named contact. Awards will be made only through the official process; applicants should never pay a fee to apply for travel support.

Questions may be sent through the conference contact form by selecting Registration.
